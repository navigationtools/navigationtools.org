GlassWorks is a small family of watch faces for people who go to sea. Each one puts on your wrist something a navigator keeps an eye on: the barometer, the sky, the tide. Everything is worked out on the watch itself, so it keeps working offshore with no phone and no signal.

They run on Garmin's larger round watches: the quatix 7 Pro and quatix 8, the fēnix 7X, fēnix 8 AMOLED and fēnix 9, the epix Pro and the MARQ Gen 3. They are free on the Connect IQ store.

## WeatherGlass: the ship's barometer on your wrist

{{< figure src="/images/weatherglass/main.png" alt="The WeatherGlass face: the pressure trace, a forecast line, and wind, sun, moon and tide marks round the edge" class="watch" >}}

The pressure and its trend, a forecast worked out from them, the sea state, the wind, the sun, the moon and the tide, on one dial. [More about WeatherGlass](/weatherglass/)

## CelestialGlass: where to look, and when

{{< figure src="/images/celestialglass/face.png" alt="The CelestialGlass face: the stars for the evening fix named round the rim, the sky as a 24-hour ring, and the time in Nixie tubes" class="watch" >}}

The picture a navigator carries in their head: which stars to shoot at twilight and where they will be, the sky as a clock, and the weather, a storm's side and the tide one press away. [More about CelestialGlass](/celestialglass/)

Both faces are by Larry Sherman, with the Clipper Race training shipmates.
