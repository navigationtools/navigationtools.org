WeatherGlass is a Connect IQ watch face built around the barometer.

## On the face

1. **The pressure trace,** coloured by the shipping forecast's terms for how fast the pressure is changing.
2. **Wind and sun** round the edge: wind direction and speed, sunrise and sunset.
3. **UTC and local time,** with the local time large.
4. **Heart rate,** coloured by your Garmin zones.
5. **A red palette** that's easy on the eyes on deck at night.
