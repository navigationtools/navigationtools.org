WeatherGlass is a watch face for sailors. The barometer and a forecast sit at the top of the dial; wind, sun, moon and tides are marks around the edge. Everything is worked out on the watch, so it works offshore with no phone and no signal.

## The barometer

1. **A 3-hour pressure trace** above today's sea-level pressure.
2. **Coloured in shipping-forecast terms** for how fast the pressure is changing: green for steady or slowly, amber for quickly, red for very rapidly.
3. **It keeps its own pressure log,** so a hike or a drive over hills doesn't show up as weather. When the trend can't be trusted, the trace turns grey and the forecast line says why.

## The forecast and the sea state

1. **A forecast line** worked out from the pressure, its trend, the wind and the season (the Zambretti method), for either hemisphere: for example SETTLED FINE, or RAIN AT TIMES, UNSETTLED.
2. **The sea state follows it** as a Douglas number, worked out from the last three days of wind and a fetch you choose: RAIN AT TIMES, UNSETTLED: D5.
3. **The sea state has its own colour:** green up to D4, amber D5, orange D6, red D7 and above. A steep, short sea shows one colour higher.

## Wind, sun, moon and tides

1. **Wind:** a triangle pointing in from where the wind blows, with the speed in knots, coloured by Beaufort force. Weather-chart barbs if you want them.
2. **Sun and moon:** sunrise, sunset, moonrise and moonset as marks at their clock positions, with the moon drawn in its phase.
3. **Tides:** choose a port for the next high and low water, with springs and neaps. 77 ports, including every Clipper Round the World Race port grouped by leg. A planning guide, not for pilotage.

## For the watch on deck

{{< figure src="/images/weatherglass/rig-for-red.png" alt="WeatherGlass in Rig for red, all in red to keep night vision" class="watch small" >}}

1. **Large local time,** with the day, date and UTC on one line.
2. **Heart rate** with a one-hour trace, plus steps and battery.
3. **Colours that read at night,** or **Rig for red** to keep your night vision.
4. **Two crew touches,** both optional: a pint at your chosen time, with your beer's name, and a V sign once a day.

## Watches

Tested on both screen types: AMOLED (quatix 7 Pro) and memory-in-pixel (fēnix 7X Sapphire Solar). Runs on the larger round AMOLED watches (quatix 7 Pro, epix Pro, fēnix 8 AMOLED, quatix 8, tactix 8, fēnix 9, MARQ Gen 3) and the fēnix 7X family.
